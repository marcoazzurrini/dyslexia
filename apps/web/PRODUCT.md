# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

One person: the owner, who finds reading hard. Only their Google account may sign in. They add article links during the day and listen mostly on the move, walking or commuting, with the phone in a pocket and only glancing at the screen.

## Product Purpose

Reader turns an article link into a narration to listen to, so reading difficulty never stands between the listener and what they want to read. Success is pasting a link, getting audio without reviewing anything, and listening to the end with the original one tap away.

## Positioning

Narration adapted for listening yet faithful to the source: a language model rewrites the text so it is easy to hear, never summarizing or changing its meaning, and the original article with its visuals is always one tap away.

## Operating Context

- Installed on an iPhone as a Home Screen app, and also used in Safari.
- Listening happens with the screen locked or the app in the background; lock-screen and headset controls matter as much as the screen.
- Narrations take a while to make, so the listener leaves and comes back.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Paste a link; the narration is being made, ready, or failed with a reason. Failed narrations can be retried or removed.
- Library filtered by not started, in progress, and finished; progress and speed are kept on this device only.
- Player with seek, speed, and lock-screen controls through Media Session.
- Google sign-in inside the app, never by emailed link.
- Articles over 100,000 extracted characters are refused.

## Brand Commitments

- The name is Reader. "Dyslexia" was a working title and should not appear to the listener.
- Copy is plain, calm, and in sentence case.

## Evidence on Hand

No testimonials, users, or metrics beyond the owner. Do not invent any.

## Product Principles

- Listening comes first; the player is always in reach.
- Faithful to the source, with the original one tap away.
- Nothing to review: a link in, a recording out.
- Every state explains what happened and what to do next.
- Feels like an iPhone app: familiar iOS patterns over invention.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The listener is dyslexic. Avoid glare and pure black text, keep running text at 1.5 line spacing or more, use sentence case, and follow the iOS text size setting. Respect reduced motion, reduced transparency, and increased contrast.
